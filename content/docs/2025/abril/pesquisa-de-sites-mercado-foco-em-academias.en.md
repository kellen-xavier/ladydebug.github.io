### Studies and general analysis of gym websites

This brief research was carried out for a website prototype in the fitness sector. Its goal was to promote a studio focused on personal goals, aimed at people who dislike crowded gyms and a lack of instructor attention, prioritizing a personal approach to service.

---

**1. Typical gym websites:**

Gym websites are generally built around three main pillars:

- **Presenting services:** showing the types of training available, such as strength training, functional training, spinning, Pilates, and yoga.
- **Attracting new members:** highlighting promotions, plans, trial classes, and quick contact forms.
- **Social proof:** displaying real photos, member testimonials, and results.

**2. Common website structure:**

- **An eye-catching homepage:** images of people training, motivational messages, and the gym's distinguishing features, such as 24-hour opening, a free fitness assessment, and parking.
- **Plans and pricing:** many people want to find the monthly price quickly. Some sites show it; others require users to fill out a form first, which can cause them to leave.
- **Group class schedule:** some gyms publish their weekly schedule on the website.
- **Location:** an embedded map, usually Google Maps, is almost essential because location is a deciding factor.
- **Member area:** some newer sites offer member logins for tracking workouts, payments, and physical progress.
- **Blog or health tips:** uncommon, but useful for SEO and attracting more visitors through Google.
- **Direct chat or WhatsApp:** many sites have floating buttons for quick WhatsApp contact.

**3. How people search for gym websites:**

- **Convenience:** people want quick information. Slow sites or excessive forms lose users.
- **Comparison:** users commonly open several nearby gym websites to compare prices, facilities, and distinguishing features.
- **Credibility:** good photos, visible facilities, and real testimonials build trust.
- **Immediate benefits:** free registration, a first-month discount, or a free trial class can be decisive.

**4. SEO and traffic:**

- Many searches use local keywords, such as "**gym in [city/neighborhood]**" or "**gym near me**".
- Websites optimized for these searches are much more likely to be found organically, without paid advertising.

**5. Design and current trends:**

- A clean, modern appearance without clutter.
- Short homepage videos showing the space.
- Integration with social media, especially Instagram.
